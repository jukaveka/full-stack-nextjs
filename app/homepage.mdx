# Blog application

Application for keeping track of interesting blogs and their reading status. 

Every blog added is visible for everyone else, allowing for sharing of interesting blogs between all users.

## Features

- Browse blogs
- Add blogs
- Follow your reading list

## Future features

- Commenting
- Suggestions
- Collections

## Getting started

1. **Create user** by [registering](/register) to the application
2. [Login](/login) to your **new user**
3. Add a new blog that you want to **read and share**
4. **Browse** blogs added by others
5. Keep up with your **reading list** on your profile page
6. When you've read blog on your list, **mark it as read**
